## Project 12 — Capstone (Month 9)

**Type:** Hybrid
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Estimated workload:** 30–35 hrs/week
**Prerequisites:** All intensive phases, Projects 7–11

### Goal

Produce a full campaign or editorial package — visual and textual — that demonstrates the full curriculum as an integrated practice. The capstone is the portfolio's centrepiece and the evidence that the director can run a project end-to-end without supervision.

### Brief

You are directing a complete creative package for a client, publication, or project of your choice. The package must include a visual component and a textual component, integrated under a single authorial position. The package must demonstrate the full pipeline: brief, specification, generation, diagnosis, curation, direction, refusal, and delivery.

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Working brief** | Intention, constraints, references, acceptance criteria, rejection conditions, risk assessment for both components |
| **Visual pipeline** | ComfyUI node graph, fully documented |
| **Writing pipeline** | Prompt chain, fully documented |
| **First sets** | 10 visual outputs and 5 drafts, each diagnosed |
| **Second sets** | 10 visual and 5 textual revised outputs, each diagnosed, with one accepted each and three rejected each, justified |
| **Final package** | Visual component (key visual, campaign assets, or image set) and textual component (editorial, campaign copy, or script), integrated |
| **Authorial position** | One page. Private and public. What is this work for? |
| **Revenue model** | Licensing, service, or hybrid. Specific mechanism. Three client profiles. |
| **Decision log** | Minimum 30 entries, each naming the decision, the level, the alternative, and the outcome |
| **Written defense** | One page, naming the visual and writing levels, the decisions, and the alternatives |
| **Refusal statement** | One page. The categories of refusal practised, with examples from the six months of work. |
| **External critique record** | One structured critique session on the full package |
| **Portfolio integration** | The capstone becomes the centrepiece of the portfolio from Phase VI |

### Package Requirements

The package must demonstrate:

- A single authorial position across visual and textual components.
- The visual and textual components share an intention.
- The components are integrated, not merely combined.
- The package can be defended as a whole, not just as parts.
- The decision log reconstructs every major decision across both components.
- The refusal statement names the boundaries of the practice.

### Pipeline Requirements

Both pipelines must be documented. The integration of the two pipelines must be explained in the decision log. The package must include at least one LoRA or IP-Adapter, at least one ControlNet conditioning layer, a voice anchor, a draft gate, and a fact gate.

### External Critique Requirements

- What is the package trying to do?
- Is the authorial position clear?
- Do the visual and textual components share an intention?
- Does the integration work, or does one component dominate?
- At what level does the package fail?
- Does the package share the AI default signature in either medium?
- Would you hire this director? Why or why not?

### Standing Inquiry Integration

- **Weekly:** One sentence in the decision log.
- **Monthly:** One page answering "What is this work for?"
- **Quarterly:** A written revision of the answer, read against previous quarterly answers.
- **Annual:** A full synthesis, read against all previous entries.

### Mastery Gate

The capstone is complete when:

- The brief scores 3 or higher on the Intent Architecture rubric.
- Both pipelines score 3 or higher on their respective rubrics.
- The authorial position scores 3 or higher on the Authorship as Position rubric.
- The package demonstrates a single authorial position across both components.
- The decision log reconstructs every major decision.
- The written defense names both levels, decisions, and alternatives.
- The refusal statement names the categories and provides examples.
- Every verifiable claim is verified.
- The external critique is logged and diagnosed.
- The capstone is integrated into the portfolio.

### Common Failure Modes

- **Two projects, not one.** The visual and textual components do not share an intention. Fix: state the intention in one sentence. If it takes two, it is two projects.
- **One component dominates.** The visual or textual component is clearly stronger. Fix: the integration must be balanced. Test: which component carries the package? If one, the integration has failed.
- **No authorial position.** The package demonstrates skill but not a position. Fix: the position must be stated and demonstrated. The portfolio is the evidence.
- **No refusal statement.** The director has not articulated their boundaries. Fix: the refusal statement is not optional. It is the evidence of a practice.
- **No external critique.** The director has not tested the package against outside eyes. Fix: the critique is required. Self-assessment alone has blind spots.
