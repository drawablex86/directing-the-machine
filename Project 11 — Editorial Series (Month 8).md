## Project 11 — Editorial Series (Month 8)

**Type:** Writing
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Estimated workload:** 20–25 hrs/week
**Prerequisites:** All intensive phases

### Goal

Produce a short-form editorial series — five pieces, one voice, one claim — using the full writing framework. The project must demonstrate that voice holds across a series, not just a single piece.

### Brief

You are directing an editorial series for a publication of your choice. The series must make a single claim across five pieces. Each piece must stand alone, but the series must build an argument. The voice must be consistent across all five pieces.

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Working brief** | Intention, constraints, references, acceptance criteria, rejection conditions, risk assessment |
| **Voice fingerprint** | Five or more specific features |
| **Pipeline documentation** | Prompt chain, with every step explained as a decision |
| **First set** | 5 drafts, each diagnosed against the brief |
| **Second set** | 5 revised drafts, each diagnosed, with one accepted and three rejected, justified |
| **Five final pieces** | 800–1,200 words each, same voice, one claim |
| **Decision log** | Minimum 20 entries |
| **Written defense** | One page, naming the writing level, the decision, and the alternative |
| **Refusal** | One writing refusal — a piece the pipeline could produce that the director refuses to deliver |
| **Fact-check record** | Every verifiable claim verified |
| **External critique record** | One structured critique session on the series |

### Series Requirements

The series must demonstrate:

- A single claim stated in the first piece and defended across all five.
- A consistent voice across all five.
- Each piece stands alone.
- The series builds an argument.
- The fifth piece resolves the claim made in the first.

### Pipeline Requirements

The writing pipeline must include:

- At least four steps (architect, drafter, editor, verifier).
- The voice anchor injected at every step.
- The output of each piece fed into the next as context.
- A draft gate and a fact gate per piece.
- Documented variables and levers.

### External Critique Requirements

- What is the claim of the series?
- Does the first piece state the claim?
- Does the fifth piece resolve it?
- Is the voice consistent across all five?
- At what level does any piece fail — argument, structure, voice, rhythm?
- Does the work share the AI default writing signature?

### Standing Inquiry Integration

- **Weekly:** One sentence in the decision log.
- **Monthly:** One page answering "What is this work for?"

### Mastery Gate

The project is complete when:

- The brief scores 3 or higher on the Intent Architecture rubric.
- The pipeline scores 3 or higher on the Writing Pipeline I rubric.
- The voice fingerprint names five or more features.
- The five pieces demonstrate the same voice and build a single claim.
- The decision log reconstructs every major decision.
- The written defense names the writing level, the decision, and the alternative.
- The refusal is specific and grounded.
- Every verifiable claim is verified.
- The external critique is logged and diagnosed.

### Common Failure Modes

- **No claim.** The series is five pieces about a topic, not five pieces arguing a claim. Fix: state the claim in one sentence. If you cannot, the series has no argument.
- **Voice drift.** The voice changes across the five pieces. Fix: re-inject the voice anchor at every step. Test the fingerprint against each piece.
- **Standalone failure.** A piece cannot be read without the others. Fix: each piece must stand alone. Test: read piece 3 first. Does it make sense?
- **Series failure.** The pieces do not build. Fix: the fifth piece must resolve the claim of the first. Test: read pieces 1 and 5 together. Does the series close?
