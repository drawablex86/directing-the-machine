## Project 8 — Writing Voice Guide (Month 5)

**Type:** Writing
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Estimated workload:** 20–25 hrs/week
**Prerequisites:** All intensive phases

### Goal

Produce a brand voice guide and three sample pieces using the full writing framework — brief, pipeline, diagnosis, curation, direction, and refusal. The project must demonstrate that the framework survives contact with a real or simulated voice brief.

### Brief

You are directing a voice guide for a brand, publication, or individual. The guide must specify the voice with enough precision that a pipeline can execute it and a human can verify it. The three sample pieces must demonstrate the voice across three different subjects, formats, or lengths.

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Working brief** | Intention, constraints, references, acceptance criteria, rejection conditions, risk assessment |
| **Voice fingerprint** | Sentence-length distribution, register, vocabulary range, syntactic habits, characteristic moves |
| **Pipeline documentation** | Prompt chain, with every step explained as a decision |
| **First set** | 5 drafts, each diagnosed against the brief |
| **Second set** | 5 revised drafts, each diagnosed, with one accepted and three rejected, justified |
| **Three sample pieces** | Same voice across three subjects, formats, or lengths |
| **Decision log** | Minimum 20 entries, each naming the decision, the level, the alternative, and the outcome |
| **Written defense** | One page, naming the writing level, the decision, and the alternative |
| **Refusal** | One writing refusal, specific, justified, grounded in a slop criterion or ethical principle |
| **Fact-check record** | Every verifiable claim verified against a primary source |
| **External critique record** | One structured critique session, with a diagnosis of the feedback against the writing ladder |

### Voice Guide Requirements

The voice guide must include:

- The voice fingerprint (five or more specific features).
- Three voice anchors (human texts, not adjectives).
- The slop criterion per ladder level.
- The fact-gate protocol.

### Pipeline Requirements

The writing pipeline must include:

- At least four steps (architect, drafter, editor, verifier).
- One voice anchor injected at every step.
- A draft gate.
- A fact gate.
- Documented variables and levers.

### Decision Log Requirements

Every decision entry must include:

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| Date | Yes |
| Decision | Yes |
| Level (writing ladder) | Yes |
| Reason | Yes |
| Alternative rejected | Yes |
| Outcome | Yes |
| Revision if failed | Yes |

### External Critique Requirements

- What does the piece seem to be trying to do?
- What is the claim?
- Is the voice consistent with the anchor?
- At what level does the work fail — argument, structure, voice, rhythm?
- Does the work share the AI default writing signature?
- What would you change if it were yours?

### Standing Inquiry Integration

- **Weekly:** One sentence in the decision log.
- **Monthly:** One page answering "What is this work for?"

### Mastery Gate

The project is complete when:

- The brief scores 3 or higher on the Intent Architecture rubric.
- The pipeline scores 3 or higher on the Writing Pipeline I rubric.
- The voice fingerprint names five or more specific features.
- The three sample pieces demonstrate the same voice across three dimensions.
- The decision log reconstructs every major decision with the alternative rejected.
- The written defense names the writing level, the decision, and the alternative.
- The refusal is specific, justified, and grounded.
- Every verifiable claim is verified.
- The external critique is logged and diagnosed.

### Common Failure Modes

- **Voice by adjective.** The student asks for "a witty, professional tone" and expects a specific voice. Fix: voice anchors are texts, not adjectives.
- **No fact gate.** The student accepts unverified claims. Fix: every verifiable claim is verified.
- **Slop drift.** The accepted piece shares the AI writing signature (hedged claims, symmetrical structure, generic voice). Fix: compare against reference-class anchors. Apply the slop criterion.
- **No refusal.** The student delivers everything the pipeline produces. Fix: identify the draft the pipeline would produce if the director did nothing. Refuse it.
- **Voice drift across samples.** The three sample pieces do not share the same voice. Fix: re-inject the voice anchor at every step. Test the fingerprint against each piece.
