## Project 9 — Hybrid Visual Essay (Month 6)

**Type:** Hybrid
**Duration:** 4 weeks
**Estimated workload:** 25–30 hrs/week
**Prerequisites:** All intensive phases

### Goal

Produce a visual essay — a hybrid form combining image and text — that argues a single claim. The project must demonstrate that the visual and writing frameworks can be integrated without one dominating the other.

### Brief

You are directing a visual essay of 5 images and 2,000 words. The images and the text must share a single argument. The images must not illustrate the text; the text must not caption the images. Both must carry the argument.

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Working brief** | Intention, constraints, references, acceptance criteria, rejection conditions, risk assessment for both visual and writing components |
| **Visual pipeline** | ComfyUI node graph, with every node explained as a decision |
| **Writing pipeline** | Prompt chain, with every step explained as a decision |
| **First set** | 10 visual outputs and 5 drafts, each diagnosed against the brief |
| **Second set** | 10 visual and 5 textual revised outputs, each diagnosed, with one accepted each and three rejected each, justified |
| **Final visual essay** | 5 images and 2,000 words, integrated |
| **Decision log** | Minimum 30 entries, each naming the decision, the level, the alternative, and the outcome |
| **Written defense** | One page, naming the visual and writing levels, the decisions, and the alternatives |
| **Refusal** | One visual refusal and one writing refusal, specific, justified, grounded |
| **External critique record** | One structured critique session on the hybrid form, with a diagnosis of the feedback against both ladders |

### Integration Requirements

The visual essay must demonstrate:

- The images and text share a claim.
- The images do not merely illustrate the text.
- The text does not merely caption the images.
- The sequence of images and text creates an argument that neither could create alone.
- The voice of the text and the visual language of the images are coherent.

### Pipeline Requirements

The visual and writing pipelines must be documented separately and integrated in the final output. The integration must be explained in the decision log.

### External Critique Requirements

- What is the essay trying to argue?
- Do the images and text share the claim?
- Does the sequence create an argument?
- At what level does the visual component fail?
- At what level does the writing component fail?
- Does the integration work, or does one medium dominate?
- Does the work share the AI default signature in either medium?

### Standing Inquiry Integration

- **Weekly:** One sentence in the decision log.
- **Monthly:** One page answering "What is this work for?"

### Mastery Gate

The project is complete when:

- The brief scores 3 or higher on the Intent Architecture rubric for both components.
- Both pipelines score 3 or higher on their respective rubrics.
- The visual essay demonstrates a shared claim.
- The decision log reconstructs every major decision across both components.
- The written defense names both levels, decisions, and alternatives.
- Both refusals are specific, justified, and grounded.
- The external critique is logged and diagnosed.

### Common Failure Modes

- **Illustration.** The images illustrate the text. Fix: the images must carry the argument independently. Test: remove the text. Does the visual sequence still argue?
- **Captioning.** The text captions the images. Fix: the text must carry the argument independently. Test: remove the images. Does the text still argue?
- **One medium dominates.** The visual or writing component is clearly stronger. Fix: the integration must be balanced. Test: which medium carries the claim? If one, the integration has failed.
- **No shared claim.** The images and text are about different things. Fix: the claim must be stated in one sentence and must be visible in both media.
